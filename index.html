Stockroom Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Stockroom (also listed as Inventory Management System) is an inventory, sales and purchasing app for small shops. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Stockroom has no servers of its own: your shop's records stay on your devices and, if you turn on Google Drive sync, in your own Google Drive.

Information you put into the app

Stockroom is offline-first. What you enter is saved on your device, and the developer cannot see it:

  • Store details: store name, tagline, phone number, address, business type, currency, tax rate and receipt footer.
  • Products and stock: names, SKUs, barcodes, categories, units, costs, prices, stock levels, reorder levels, stock movements and product photos.
  • Transactions: sales and invoices, purchases and purchase orders, payments, and amounts customers owe (credit balances).
  • Customers and suppliers: names, contact people, phone numbers, email and postal addresses, tax IDs, credit limits, notes and their transaction history.

Signing in

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app. Stockroom keeps your name and email on your device to show which account you're using. If you create an account with an email address, the app keeps that email and your name on the device; your password is only checked and is not stored. Nothing about your account is sent to the developer.

Google Drive sync

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings. When it's on, Stockroom saves a copy of your shop's records, store details and product photos in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your other devices signed in to the same Google account can read them. The app can only see the files it created in that folder; it cannot see or change anything else in your Drive. Stockroom requests only the drive.appdata permission for this.

Stockroom's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan product barcodes and QR codes, and to take product photos. Barcodes are read on your device by Google ML Kit; images are not uploaded for this. ML Kit may send Google basic information about how the scanning library performs, but not your images.
  • Photos: when you pick a product photo from your gallery, a copy is saved inside the app. Stockroom does not read any other photos.
  • Internet: for Sign in with Google and Google Drive sync. The app works without it.
  • Vibration: to confirm a successful scan.

Receipts and statements print through Android's own print service, and exported files (PDF and CSV) go only where you choose to send or save them.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive, as described above, only when you use them.
  • Google ML Kit barcode scanning, which runs on your device.

Stockroom contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Sharing

Stockroom does not sell your information or share it with advertisers. Information leaves your device only when you choose to: through Google Drive sync, or when you export or share a document such as a receipt or a CSV file.

Your data: ownership, keeping and deleting

You own your business data. It stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. You can delete individual records in the app; clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app removes everything stored on that device. To remove the synced copy, open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings, then Manage apps, find Stockroom and choose Delete hidden app data. You can turn off sync in the app's Settings at any time; signing out also turns it off on that device.

Children

Stockroom is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
